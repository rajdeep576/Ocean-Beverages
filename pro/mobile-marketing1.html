Mobile Marketing Mastery

Reaching customers where they spend 80% of their time — on their phones

Types of Mobile Marketing Strategies

1. App-based Marketing

Aura Jewels runs beautiful mobile ads inside popular apps using Google AdMob and Facebook. Our app poster shows how customers can discover exclusive jewelry collections directly on their phones.

2. In-game Mobile Marketing

In-game ads appear while users play mobile games. Watch how a beautiful Aura Jewels advertisement pops up during gameplay.

3. QR Codes

Users scan QR codes to instantly access special offers, product pages, or virtual try-on features.

4. Location-based Marketing

Ads shown only when users are near our store or event location.

5. Mobile Search Ads

Google search ads optimized for mobile with click-to-call and maps.

6. Mobile App

Our own dedicated Aura Jewels app for loyalty rewards, virtual try-on, and exclusive collections.

Why SMS Campaigns Are Important

SMS campaigns achieve a remarkable 98% open rate and 45% response rate.

They create a strong sense of urgency — perfect for limited-time jewelry offers, flash sales, and exclusive drops.
